## Final generation prompt

Use case: stylized-concept. Asset type: premium dark-fantasy game website hero artwork for Sinix Grimm, an Undead Horde Rogue in World of Warcraft: Forever. Generate a striking, richly painted cinematic illustration with Warcraft-like heroic fantasy proportions and exquisite leather/metal detail. One male Forsaken undead assassin with pallid gray bone skin, a gaunt partly skeletal face and exposed angular jaw, intense glowing green eyes under a deep dark hood, layered weathered black and dark olive leather armor, restrained tarnished brass fittings, two clearly distinct daggers held naturally in anatomically coherent hands with correct grips, faint toxic green poison glint on the blades. He stands in three-quarter view in the ruined stone arches of Lordaeron with subtle green mist, dangerous and composed. Wide landscape banner composition approximately 2.5:1: character occupies the right half, head fully inside frame, dagger silhouettes readable, substantial quiet near-black olive space on the left for website typography. Chiaroscuro, precise emerald rim light, pale bone facial highlight, believable material roughness, atmospheric depth, professional AAA fantasy key art, painterly realism rather than a photo. Palette charcoal, deep forest green, muted antique gold, subtle poison green. Avoid text, letters, logos, watermarks, UI, multiple characters, extra limbs, merged weapons, modern clothing, glowing plastic armor, exaggerated spiky shoulders, excessive fog obscuring the face. No gore.
